# 01 / Product Vision & Mission

## 1.1 Vision Statement

Fast Credit Deal exists to become the most trusted loan restructuring partner for salaried Indians trapped in high EMI burdens — delivering measurable financial relief through a personalised, WhatsApp-first acquisition and conversion engine.

## 1.2 Mission

Build a predictable, scalable loan acquisition engine that consistently generates and converts high-intent borrowers into disbursals.

**Full Funnel Ownership:**

1. Content — attract high-intent borrowers
2. Lead Capture — EMI analysis tool
3. Qualification — structured scoring
4. Conversion — WhatsApp nurture
5. Disbursal — bank/NBFC partnerships

## 1.3 Core Problem Being Solved

### Customer Problem

* ₹50,000–₹1.5L+ EMIs
* 3–6 active loans
* No trusted advisor
* Agents are transactional, not helpful

### Business Problem

* No owned lead system
* Dependency on third-party leads
* No control over quality or scale

## 1.4 Operating Principle

> "This is a performance-driven loan distribution machine."

**Rule:**
If it doesn't improve:

* Lead quality
* Conversion rate
* Disbursal volume

👉 It does NOT get built.

# 02 / Ideal Customer Profile

## 2.1 Primary ICP — Salaried EMI Overload

### Demographics

* Age: 25–45
* Salary: ₹40K–₹2L
* Location: Tier 1 & 2 cities

### Financial Profile

* EMI: ₹50K–₹1.5L+
* Loans: 2–6
* CIBIL: 650–780
* EMI ratio: 40–70%

### Psychographics

* Knows problem exists
* Doesn't know solution
* Distrusts agents
* Responds to proof

### Trigger Events

* Salary realization
* Loan rejection
* Saw relatable ad

## 2.2 Disqualified Profiles

| Profile             | Reason         |

| Self-employed       | Outside niche  |
